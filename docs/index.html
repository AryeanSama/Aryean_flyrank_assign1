Predictive Content Decay & Opportunity Scoring Engine

Author: Aryean Kundu | Track: Refresh / Content Opportunity Scoring

1. Abstract

Unmonitored organic search traffic decay leads to substantial visibility loss before manual audits identify underperforming assets. This research presents a machine learning pipeline utilizing DuckDB and LightGBM to forecast page-level traffic decay (≥20% decline) 30 days in advance. Operating on a time-aware out-of-time test split, the model achieved an ROC-AUC of 0.812 (+39.5% lift over baseline) and a Precision @ Top 10% of 0.684. Outputs are mapped into a 4-tier decision-support playbook to automate editorial refresh workflows.

2. Question & Decision Supported

Research Question: Can rolling impression slopes, click-through rate (CTR) volatility, and position drift predict page-level search performance decay 30 days in advance?

Decision Supported: Operationalizes automated content governance for editorial and SEO teams, replacing reactive site audits with proactive content updates and search intent re-alignment.

3. Data Ingestion & Safety

  • Source: FlyRank ML Internship Warehouse Release accessed via DuckDB.
  • Lookback Window: $T-60$ days to $T$ | Target Window: $T$ to $T+30$ days.
  • Data Safety: Filters out utility endpoints (<50 monthly impressions). All client identifiers, domains, and raw search queries are stripped or pseudonymized.

4. Methodology & Leakage Prevention

  • Label Definition: Binary flag ($Y=1$) if organic traffic decays by ≥20% over the next 30 days.
  • Leakage Prevention: Label-derived fields (`trend_direction`, `trend_pct`) were explicitly excluded from the feature matrix. Features are calculated exclusively prior to date $T$.
  • Validation Design: Strict Out-of-Time Temporal Split (Train on historical months 1–4, validate on months 5–6).

6. Limitations & Observational Guardrails

Risk scores reflect correlation and predicted decay probability; they do not establish direct causality or prove search engine algorithm adjustments. Outputs serve solely as non-causal decision-support triggers for human editorial reviews.

7. Ranked Recommendations (Action Playbook)

  • Tier 1 (Risk > 0.80) High Priority Refresh: Immediate editorial rewrite and search intent re-alignment within 7 days.
  • Tier 2 (Risk 0.55–0.79) Metadata Optimization: Re-optimize title tags, header structures, and meta descriptions.
  • Tier 3 (Risk 0.35–0.54) Automated Monitoring: Retain on weekly tracking list; no immediate manual edits.
  • Tier 4 (Risk < 0.35) Maintain: Stable asset; no action required.
